A FEW HELPFUL DETAILS

Good to know.

A little clarity makes choosing easier. Here’s how the Sri Laxmi Silver shop works today.

Are the sample products available to buy?

No. Listings marked “Sample” or “Not for sale” are examples only and cannot be added to the bag. Please message the store to ask about current products.

How do I confirm an online order?

For available priced items, submit your details at checkout and send the prepared order message to Sri Laxmi Silver on WhatsApp. Your order is not confirmed until the store replies and confirms availability and final charges.

When should my delivery arrive?

Delivery is currently offered around 5 km from the store. The estimate is within 7 days, not a guaranteed date. The store confirms whether your address is serviceable and the delivery charge before the order is final.

Can I pay online?

Online payment is not available on this website yet. Contact Sri Laxmi Silver directly to confirm payment arrangements before placing an order.

Do I need an account to order?

Create an account or sign in before checkout. Your submitted orders are listed in My orders.

How do coupons and reviews work?

Use a current coupon code at checkout. Reviews are available for fulfilled purchases and appear only after the store approves them.

What is the returns or exchange policy?

A returns and exchanges policy has not been published here. Please ask the store about its terms before confirming your purchase.
